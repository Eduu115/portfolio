Production grade

Near-black canvas, one signal-orange accent used sparingly, condensed heavy display type over a calm grotesque, and a monospace voice for system facts. Structure borrowed from the ClickHouse DESIGN.md (awesome-design-md): flat surfaces, hairlines, no shadows, accent at element level and once as a full band.
